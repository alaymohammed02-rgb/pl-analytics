# PL Analytics: working notes

A static football analytics site on GitHub Pages. A Python sync (`sync.py`, run by `.github/workflows/sync.yml` on a schedule and on demand) fetches PitchAPI and FPL data into `data/` and generates `index.html`. Feature pages are plain JavaScript files in `assets/`, loaded after the main inline script.

## Rules agreed with the owner

- **Talk to the owner in Arabic** (Gulf Arabic is fine). Code, commits and PR descriptions are in English.
- **When the owner asks for a feature:** build it, test everything, merge it yourself, run the sync, then check the published files.
- **Never claim untested success.** Report honest results, including when a model or idea does not work. Pages must say so too.
- **Do not invent statistics or missing values.** Estimates are labelled as estimates on the page, with their tested error.
- **Secrets:**
  - never expose, request or change API keys or secrets without need;
  - no secret goes in browser JavaScript;
  - keys live in GitHub secrets (`PITCHAPI_KEY`).
- **Leave existing features alone:**
  - do not change login, permissions or the sync mechanism broadly;
  - do not remove existing features;
  - keep the site working on GitHub Pages.
- **StatsBomb Open Data terms:**
  - analysis only;
  - no redistribution of raw data;
  - no commercial use;
  - show the StatsBomb logo where it is used.
  - SkillCorner asks for credit.
- **Git:**
  - no force push;
  - stage specific files, never `git add -A` (tests touch `data/index.json`);
  - no model names in commits or PRs.

## How a feature page works

- **Registration:** add the file name to `FEATURES_JS` in `sync.py`, and **raise `FEATURES_V`** whenever an asset or `assets/fun.css` changes, because browsers cache by that version. Then rebuild `index.html` with `sh tests/regen.sh`.
- **Page setup:** each page calls `INS.addPage(key, name, section, after)` and ends with `if(typeof D==='object'&&D&&typeof route==='function')route();`. The sections are `home`, `league`, `players`, `analysis` and `fpl`.
- **Arabic:**
  - `two(en, ar)` makes paired spans (`.fx-en` / `.fx-ar`);
  - `nb(x)` wraps numbers and Latin text in Arabic sentences (`<bdi dir="ltr">`);
  - `nm(name)` wraps names;
  - `INS.ar({...})` adds translations for plain text nodes;
  - write ranges in words ("من 37 إلى 38"), because RTL reverses "37–38".
- **Async safety:** capture `D.id` before any `await`, and bail out if `root.isConnected` is false or `D.id` changed. Caches must not keep a failed result, and must be keyed by `INS.poolKey()` (data version).
- **Liquid Glass** (`assets/liquid-glass.js`, styles at the end of `assets/fun.css`): a true lens on the chosen button of the Fantasy tabs and the menu drawer.
  - The lens shows what is really under it: a copy of the group (button backgrounds, every letter, or every word in Arabic so letters stay joined, and the drawer's section titles and icons), each piece placed where the browser drew the real one (a `Range` per letter). The copy is drawn on the colour behind the group, so the real labels never show twice. It is rebuilt when the buttons, their text, the theme (`html` style/class), the language or the fonts change; colours are read with the buttons' own transitions off.
  - Pieces are `<i>` with the text in `data-c` and shown by `::before`, so `textContent`/`innerText` of the page are unchanged.
  - The glass is a spring (no fixed animation): a new choice sends it straight to the new button in any direction (about 0.35 s, small overshoot), stretching along its way; a new choice while it moves bends the path. The lens strength and the letter weight (a stroke, so nothing reflows) follow its speed; each letter is moved out by `1+A(1-d²)²` and scaled by the local stretch on each axis, so rim letters are squeezed, never overlap. Nearby buttons grow a little (Dock), the new label's letters land one by one.
  - Groups (`SP` in the file; owner asked 29 Oct 2026 that every button get the glass): the Fantasy tabs, the menu drawer, the tab strips of Hidden Gems, Player DNA, Reality Check, Team DNA (`.ins-tabs`), the Intelligence Lab (`.labtabs`) and Pressing maps (`.sbp-tabs`), the bar of pages above every page (`#hubbar`: one row, or `.hb-g` and `.hb-p`, the two rows of the Tactics hub), the level bar (`.lv-bar`), the Overview's day strip (`.mb-days`), the segmented choices (`.seg`, which includes `.hchips`; chosen = class `on` or `aria-pressed=true`) and the simulator's home / draw / away choice (`.sm-fb`). **Every bar on a page has its own glass**: the n-th match of a selector is the n-th instance (up to 24), and a bar the page draws again keeps its instance, so the glass slides to the new choice. A bar whose buttons are other ones now (`set` = the keys of its buttons: the pages of another group, another level) gets its glass on the chosen button at once, with no slide. Single actions (save, share, example chips, league links) are not groups and get none; a new choice group is one line in `SP` (`kids(tag)`, `picked`). The check that runs on every change of the page must not force a layout (`sigOf` reads text only; widths come from the ResizeObserver): a layout read while a page is being redrawn clamped the scroll position (the Lab test caught it). A link in a bar is not dragged by the browser (`dragstart` is cancelled), so the glass can be. `LGX.pose(id,x,y)` parks the first glass of a kind for tests.
  - Press-and-drag (`drag:true`, every tab bar except the drawer): press the chosen tab to lift the glass, drag it (mouse or finger; `touch-action:none` on the chosen tab only) and let go to choose the tab under it; the browser's click after a drag is swallowed. A mouse over another tab makes the glass lean 5 px towards it.
  - The buttons are untouched: the glass is `aria-hidden` with pointer events off; the attribute `data-lgx-act` marks the chosen one, never a class (tests compare `className`). A group with one button gets no glass; reduced motion jumps, without lens, lift or drag.
  - The drawer closes once the glass arrives (`lgxHold()` in `assets/drawer.js`, from the spring's predicted arrival); the page itself opens at once.
  - More groups: add `{id, sel, items, on}` (and `rebuild`, `deco`, `drag`) to `SP`. Tests: `tests/lg/ui_lg.js` (Fantasy tabs, drawer), `tests/lg/ui_lg2.js` (the other bars).

  - **Club Elo from results** (`sync_elo2` in `sync.py`, between the `ELO2 START/END` markers; `data/elo_own.json`; page `assets/club-elo.js`, `#/elo` in the Teams hub; owner asked 25 Oct 2026 to solve Elo "even from another site"): ClubElo never answered from the runners (HTTP 502 and timeouts) and cannot be reached from the sandbox, so the ratings are computed from PitchAPI's own results, per league, from the chain of finished seasons that ends at the current one (Premier League from 2021-22, the other five from 2024-25; a missing season stops the chain). K 30, home advantage 50, goal-difference multiplier 1 / 1.5 / (11+gd)/8, 10% pulled to the league mean each new season (from 1 July), a new club starts at the league mean; win/draw/loss probabilities by an ordered logit on the rating gap (s 175, c 0.55). **The settings were fitted only on matches before 1 Aug 2025** (`python3 tools/elo/fit.py --fit`: train Brier 0.591) and the sync's backtest asks every later match before it is played: **2,496 matches, Brier 0.590 against 0.649 for the league win/draw/loss rates, 51.7% right**. On the 410-ish current-season matches that have bookmaker odds, Elo scores Brier 0.568: within noise of Model 2 (0.580; Elo is lower by 0.012 per match) and within noise of the bookmakers (0.562; Elo is higher by 0.006). Ratings of different leagues are NOT comparable (each starts at 1500) and the five leagues with two seasons have a rougher start than the Premier League. The file holds, per club of the current season, the rating, the change over the last 5 and 10 matches and its last 60 ratings, plus the pre-match probabilities of every match this season (the Model report card scores them as a row, no leave-one-out needed). Recomputed only when the number of matches changes. The ClubElo step (`sync_elo`) is still tried in its 30-minute window; if it ever works, `data/elo.json` appears and nothing reads it yet. `elo_own` is in `psCurOnly`. Tests: `tests/elo/t_elo.py` (worked by hand, then every club of the real file against a second implementation), `tests/elo/ui_elo.js`, and the Elo row in `tests/nf/ui_rc.js`.
  - **Model against the market** (card at the end of `#/reportcard`, `assets/report-card.js`): the leave-one-out model, the de-vigged closing odds and the league base rates on the same finished matches, Brier and accuracy per league, with the paired difference judged against its standard error ("within noise" under 1.96 SE). First result (24 Oct 2026, 410 matches): the bookmakers beat the model, Brier 0.564 against 0.601, a gap beyond noise. The odds file has only played matches, so there is no coming-fixtures comparison; `market.json`/`elo.json` are current-season only (`psCurOnly`). A same-score match needs one agreeing team name, because a match missing from PitchAPI once took another game's odds.
- **Stat stability** (`assets/stat-stability.js`, `#/stability`, in the Players hub after Hidden Gems; numbers in `data/stability.json`, written by `sync_stability` between the `STABILITY START/END` markers in `sync.py`, recomputed only when a full past season's file version changes): for each league with two or more full past seasons, per stat, the Spearman correlation of a player's per-90 number from one season to the next (900+ minutes in both, each number first set against players of his position that season, z-score within DF / MF / FW) and an odd-vs-even-matches reliability within a season (300+ minutes per half, Spearman-Brown). First result (24 Oct 2026, Premier League 2021-22 to 2025-26, 420 pairs): passes 0.86, shots 0.71, xG 0.58, goals 0.42, assists 0.26 (raw, all positions mixed: passes 0.90, shots 0.91, goals 0.69). - **Who keeps it up?** (`assets/keeps-up.js`, `#/keepsup`, in the Players hub after Stat stability; numbers in `data/regress.json`, written offline by `python3 tools/regress/fit.py`, not by the sync, so refit it by hand when more seasons are in): the likely next-season per-90 number = position mean + (number − mean) × m ÷ (m + K), m = minutes, mean = that league's own mean for the position this season (450+ minutes, goalkeepers left out), K fitted per stat on Premier League pairs 2021-22 to 2024-25. Tested on 670 players of the six leagues, 2024-25 to 2025-26 (450+ minutes, then 900+), against "next season = this season": squared error down 30% for goals, 23% xG, 29% non-penalty xG, 14% shots, 35% assists, 28% xAG, 19% key passes, 20% tackles, 22% interceptions, all beyond noise; **passes: no gain** (the page says so). Not in the guess: moves, injuries, age, role; it says nothing about players who stop playing. Tests: `tests/rg/t_rg.py` (the file worked again with a second implementation), `tests/rg/ui_rg.js`.
- **Goalkeepers** (`assets/goalkeepers.js`, `#/keepers`, in the Defence hub after Pressing maps; the repeat test in `data/gk_repeat.json`, written offline by `python3 tools/gk/fit.py`): goals prevented = xGOT (PitchAPI's `xo` on shots on target) minus goals conceded, over the shots a keeper faced (a goal, own goals left out, or a saved attempt that was not blocked: `bl`). The keeper is the starting goalkeeper (slot 11) of the side that did not shoot; where a shot has its own keeper id (2024-25 on) the two agree on 99.5% of shots (5906 of 5937). Each keeper's number comes with the noise luck alone gives it (√Σ p(1−p)) and a reading "within noise". **Result (24 Oct 2026): it does not repeat.** Premier League 2021-22 to 2025-26: year to year Spearman −0.03 ± 0.14 over 54 keeper pairs (60+ shots both seasons), odd against even matches −0.03 ± 0.10 over 100 keeper-seasons; the sample cannot rule out a correlation up to about 0.25. The page says so. Only the Premier League has five seasons with line-ups, so the repeat test is Premier League only; the table works for any league and season that has shots and line-ups (a light past season has no line-ups and says so). `AttemptSaved` includes blocked shots (no keeper): counting them as faced halves the apparent keeper coverage. Tests: `tests/gk/t_gk.py` (the file worked again with a second implementation), `tests/gk/ui_gk.js`.

- **Transfermarkt ages and values** (`sync_tm` in `sync.py`, between the `TRANSFERMARKT START/END` markers, wrapped like the other optional steps; `data/tm.json`; test `tests/tm/t_tm.py`; owner chose this source, 24 Oct 2026): from the open **transfermarkt-datasets** players table (`https://pub-e682421888d945d684bcae8890b0ec20.r2.dev/data/players.csv.gz`, CC0, by dcaribou; we never scrape transfermarkt.com, whose terms forbid it). **That dataset's updates are paused: it is current to 6 July 2026** (the file records the download's `Last-Modified` as `asof` and the page prints it), so values are dated and later transfers are missing. Players are matched by name only (accents, ø, ł folded; word order ignored; one active row with that name = a match; the same name twice is settled by the club's words being inside the other's, then by the position, else left out as ambiguous; since match version 2 also "one name inside the other" ("Victor Nilsson Lindelöf" / "Victor Lindelöf") and one-word rows ("Alisson Becker" / "Alisson"), both only when the club agrees and exactly one row fits; an age outside 15–46 is left out): no fuzzy matching, no guess. `TM_MATCH_V` is stored in the file as `mv`: changing the rules re-downloads and re-matches in the next window instead of waiting 7 days. About 180 of the 530 unmatched domestic players were at newly promoted clubs (Málaga, Deportivo, Paderborn, Elversberg, Troyes, Le Mans, Monza, Frosinone, Marítimo, Académico Viseu, Schalke) whose players the dataset does not have: no matching rule can fix those. The file holds `[date of birth, value in euros]` per PitchAPI player id plus the counts (matched / ambiguous / unmatched / implausible, per league). Downloaded once every 7 days, in a 30-minute window every 3 hours. `data/tm.json` is committed as the empty placeholder `{"t":"","p":{}}` so the page's request does not 404 (a 404 is a console error that fails the page tests); the step treats it as no file. Neither the host nor the CSV columns could be reached from the sandbox (the columns are assumed from the dataset's documentation: `name`, `date_of_birth`, `market_value_in_eur`, `current_club_name`, `last_season`): **First real run (25 Oct 2026 00:30 UTC): the download and the columns worked; 22,292 active rows, 2,627 of 3,239 PitchAPI players matched (81%: Premier League 397/449, LaLiga 385/475, Bundesliga 321/400, Ligue 1 322/418, Serie A 398/478, Liga Portugal 307/441, World Cup 931/1,028), 32 ambiguous, 580 not found by name.** The file's `Last-Modified` was 5 Sep 2026, while the project's page said its data is current to 6 July 2026: the page prints both, because the file date may overstate how fresh the values are. Unmatched names are the next thing to improve (the log line gives counts only).

The page says leagues are treated as equal, the model ignores positions, and only real XIs were tested. ## Data facts that matter

- **Shots** (`*_sh.json`) carry goal minutes:
  - `ev === 'Goal'`;
  - `og` marks an own goal, which counts for the other side;
  - stoppage time is in `ma`, so sort by `mn` then `ma`.
  - The goal timeline adds up to the final score in every match checked.
- **Line-ups** (`*_mx.json`): `xi` entries are `[id, name, shirt, slot, depth, lateral, captain]`.
  - Slot 11 is the goalkeeper.
  - Slots 30–39 are the defensive line; centre-backs are the ones with lateral between 0.15 and 0.85.
  - A match can have a line-up but no player rows (status "partial").
- **Player rows** (`*_pl.json`): `xag` is missing for whole matches, not per player.
  - `duw` (duels won) does not include aerial duels: it is lower than `aerw` in about a third of player-matches, so "duels won − aerials won" is not ground duels (the Defensive table's "Ground won" column still derives it that way; reported to the owner, 23 Oct 2026).
  - `tko`/`tkw` are take-ons, not tackles (`tk`).
  - `*_px.json.gz` / `*_pxa.json` (recoveries, touches…) are old files the sync no longer writes (last 19 Oct 2026); nothing on the site reads them.
- **Heat maps** (`*_heat.json`): 16 cells along the pitch. A player's average position is the action-weighted cell centre (10+ actions).
- **Defensive line** (`data/dlh.json`, built offline by `tools/line-height/`):
  - StatsBomb 360 lines, corrected by a SkillCorner-trained model (0.31 m error);
  - plus the current-season estimate formula and its validation.

- **Past seasons** (`data/seasons.json`, sync section "المواسم السابقة"):
  - Finished seasons from PitchAPI under `<league>_<season>` (e.g. `data/l_4WFCIZ_2025-26_sh.json`), same formats as the current files.
  - Each match is fetched once; at most 1200 PitchAPI requests per sync run (`PS_REQ_PER_RUN`); jobs in `PS_JOBS`.
  - Full seasons cost 10 requests a match and about 49 KB; light seasons (matches, player rows, shots) cost 5 and about 16 KB.
  - PitchAPI lists 2021-22 to 2026-27 for all six leagues; the plan allows past seasons (measured 23 Oct 2026).
  - `data/index.json → seasons` lists the full past seasons per league (light ones are left out). Light seasons have team ids, not names, in `c`; the names are in the `team` rows.
  - Premier League 2021-22 to 2024-25 were fetched light, then switched to full (PR #52, 23 Oct 2026): the sync adds only the missing parts (advanced stats, heat maps, line-ups, ratings, halves), about 120 matches a run, before the other leagues' 2025-26. Until then the banner says the extras are still being added.
  - **PitchAPI has no player rows for every league-season** (measured 24 Oct 2026): Ligue 1 2021-22 and 2022-23 came back with matches and shots only (3 of 5 endpoints "not found"); that cost about 3600 requests before it was noticed. A new light season now starts with a probe (`PS_PROBE` = 6 matches): if none has advanced player rows, nothing is published and it is retried after `PS_PLAN_RETRY_D` days. Light seasons 2024-25 for LaLiga, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Serie A and Liga Portugal are queued in `PS_JOBS` (for the Stat stability page); `sync_stability` reads `data/seasons.json` (so light seasons count) and leaves out a season whose player file has no rows. The partial Ligue 1 2021-22 and 2022-23 files in `data/` are leftovers of that attempt and nothing reads them.
- **Season selector** (`assets/seasons.js` + `psView` inline): a past season keeps the league id and swaps the league's files.
  - A switch reloads the page.
  - Pages that need this season's Fantasy data, fixtures or live data are listed in `NOT` and say "not available for a past season".
  - Files that describe only the current season are answered "not found" in a past season (`psCurOnly`).

## Testing and merging

- **Tests:** see `tests/README.md`. They run on every PR (`.github/workflows/tests.yml`); a new test must be added to a job list there. Each page has a test that recomputes its numbers independently from `data/`, and checks Arabic, a 390 px phone, leaving before the data arrives, and a failing file.
- **Flow:** PR → merge → run the `sync.yml` workflow → compare the files on `main` (raw.githubusercontent.com) with the local copies → check that the Pages deployment succeeded. The published `github.io` site cannot be reached from the cloud sandbox.
- **Pitfalls:**
  - `pkill -f` with a pattern that matches your own command kills your shell: use `pgrep`, then `kill`.
  - The photo CDN is unreachable in the sandbox.
